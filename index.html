Original prompts from Brian. Replace the placeholders, then review every output before you send or decide.

1:1 prep brief

Turn scattered notes into a sharp agenda without inventing performance judgments.

Copy this prompt

You are helping a manager prepare for a weekly 1:1. Using only the notes I paste below, produce: (1) 3 open commitments with owners and dates if mentioned, (2) 3 questions I should ask, (3) 1 risk or blocker to address, (4) a 4-bullet proposed agenda. Do not invent facts, praise, or criticism that is not supported by the notes. Flag anything unclear as a question for me.

Notes:
{{paste notes}}

Meeting notes → actions

Convert a transcript or rough notes into owners and due dates you can send for confirmation.

Copy this prompt

Convert the meeting notes below into an action list. For each item include: action, owner (or 'unassigned'), due date if stated, and status (new / needs confirmation). List open questions separately. Do not assign owners or dates that were not said. End with a short follow-up email draft asking people to confirm their items.

Meeting notes:
{{paste notes}}

Delegation brief for an AI agent

Brief AI the way you would brief a new hire—goal, constraints, definition of done, and checks.

Copy this prompt

Rewrite my rough task into a clear delegation brief for an AI agent with these sections: Objective, Context, Constraints (what must not change), Definition of done, Required outputs, Checks to run before claiming finished, and Escalation rules (when to stop and ask a human). Keep it under 250 words. Use only what I provide.

Rough task:
{{describe task}}

Weekly status from raw updates

Draft a status update for stakeholders while keeping escalation decisions with you.

Copy this prompt

Using only the team updates below, draft a weekly status with sections: Done, In progress, Blocked, Asks, and Risks. Keep each bullet factual and short. Mark items that need my judgment with [REVIEW]. Do not soften bad news or invent metrics.

Updates:
{{paste updates}}

SOP from a walkthrough

Turn a recorded process into a draft SOP you still approve before it becomes team truth.

Copy this prompt

Turn this walkthrough into a step-by-step SOP for a teammate doing the task for the first time. Include: purpose, when to use it, prerequisites, numbered steps, common mistakes, and a short 'done when' checklist. Call out any step that needs human judgment or access I did not describe. Do not invent tools or shortcuts I did not mention.

Walkthrough:
{{paste transcript or outline}}

Decision memo (options + recommendation)

Force a clear recommendation with assumptions and kill criteria—useful for managers and owners alike.

Copy this prompt

Write a one-page decision memo from the facts below. Structure: Decision needed, Options (2–4), Pros/cons for each, Recommendation, Key assumptions, What would change my mind, and Next step if we approve. Do not invent data. If information is missing, list it under Open questions instead of guessing.
